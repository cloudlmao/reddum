# R3DDUM

A fast-paced tower defense game where strategic placement and timing matter more than brute force.

## About

R3DDUM puts you in command of a defense network against waves of increasingly dangerous enemies. Build towers, upgrade your arsenal, and chain together kills to maximize your score. Each tower type serves a distinct purpose—freezing enemies in place, melting through armor, or chaining lightning between groups.

The game rewards aggressive play. Kill enemies quickly to build combos and earn bonus gold. Position towers to create synergies—a Cryo Tower slowing enemies while a Marksman picks them off, or Tesla arcs jumping between a cluster frozen in place.

## Towers

**Sentry** — Your bread and butter. Reliable damage at a fair price.

**Marksman** — Long range, devastating single-target damage. Limited placement means you need to choose positions carefully.

**Gatling** — High rate of fire, low damage per shot. Shreds through groups of weak enemies.

**Cryo Tower** — Freezes enemies, slowing them to a crawl. Pairs well with everything.

**Mortar** — Explosive area damage. Overkill for small foes, essential for swarms.

**Laser Beam** — Continuous damage that ignores armor. Burns through tanks.

**Poison Emitter** — Damage over time that spreads between nearby enemies.

**Tesla Coil** — Chain lightning that jumps between targets. Devastating against clustered groups.

## Enemies

Standard **Grunts** make up the bulk of each wave. **Dashers** move fast but die easy. **Juggernauts** absorb punishment and keep walking. **Overlords** appear as wave bosses—massive health pools requiring focused fire.

Support enemies change how you approach each wave. **Menders** heal nearby units. **Sentinels** carry shields that absorb hits. **Hydras** split into smaller enemies on death. **Hatchlings** spawn in overwhelming numbers.

## Difficulty

**Easy** — Fewer waves, enemies hit softer. Learn the ropes.

**Medium** — Standard experience. Waves introduce new enemy types gradually.

**Hard** — More enemies, faster spawns, tougher bosses. Requires solid strategy.

**Endless** — No final wave. Survive as long as you can. Leaderboard rankings.

## Controls

| Action | Input |
|--------|-------|
| Place tower | Left click (after selecting from shop) |
| Select tower | Left click on placed tower |
| Move tower | Click and drag (between waves only) |
| Upgrade tower | Click upgrade buttons on selected tower |
| Cancel selection | Right click or ESC |
| Speed up game | Spacebar |
| Pause | ESC |

Number keys 1-8 quick-select tower types from the shop.

## Features

- 8 distinct tower types with unique abilities
- 9 enemy types with special behaviors
- Tower synergy system for combo bonuses
- Wave modifiers that change enemy properties
- Kill feed and combo meter for tracking performance
- Post-processing effects with adjustable graphics settings
- Floating damage numbers and screen shake feedback
